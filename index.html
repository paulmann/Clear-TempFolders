Clear-TempFolders

TEMP folders back to zero, safely

PowerShell cleanup for Windows TEMP folders: dry-run, age threshold, locked-file skipping, colour summary and a weekly Task Scheduler job. Works on PowerShell 5.1 and 7.

What it does

  • Dry-run shows what would be deleted
  • Skips files that are locked or in use
  • Registers a weekly Task Scheduler job
